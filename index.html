Nine machines, pallet to pack

The line in the order a can meets it. Open any machine in the 3D explorer to see it running, or take it apart to see how it goes together.

  1. 1

    Shaker table

    468 empties per pallet layer

    A pallet of empty cans rides a scissor lift up to the vibrating deck. Each layer is swept toward the funnel, where cans single-file onto the gravity track.

    Explore in 3DTake it apart
  2. 2

    Gravity track and ionized-air rinse

    68 cm twist, upside down and back

    Empties roll down the chute into a twist cage that turns each can upside down, blasts it with ionized air from the air knife, then turns it back upright onto the infeed.

    Explore in 3DTake it apart
  3. 3

    Datecoder

    One lot and date code every 2.4 s

    An inkjet head on its own stand prints the lot and date code on every can as it passes on the 2 ft infeed.

    Explore in 3DTake it apart
  4. 4

    Cask mACS canner

    3 heads, 25 cans/min, 63 cases an hour

    Three heads purge each can with CO2 and fill it. The nitrodoser adds a drop of liquid nitrogen, a lid drops from the 9 ft stack, and the cam-driven seamer closes the can with a double seam.

    Explore in 3DTake it apart
  5. 5

    9 ft outfeed conveyor

    9.4 ft at 12 cm/s to the tunnel

    Carries sealed cans west to the tunnel, where they stop against the dead plate and form rows of 12.

    Explore in 3DTake it apart
  6. 6

    Tunnel of Love pasteurizer

    Rows of 12, 22 min through

    A pusher loads each row of 12 onto the belt. Spray headers heat, hold and cool the cans in three zones, fed by zone pumps, a water heater and a plate heat exchanger on the utility skid.

    Explore in 3DTake it apart
  7. 7

    Double-wide conveyor

    5 ft of accumulation to the labeler

    Takes pasteurized cans from the tunnel discharge and meters them toward the labeler.

    Explore in 3DTake it apart
  8. 8

    PL-501 wrap labeler

    79 in long at true size

    A spacing wheel gaps the cans, the applicator peels each label off the web, and the wrap pad and hold-down belt roll it on. The liner rewinds on the second spindle.

    Explore in 3DTake it apart
  9. 9

    Rotary collection table

    108 cm turntable

    A slow turntable gathers labeled cans inside its guide rail so they can be packed by hand.

    Explore in 3DTake it apart
