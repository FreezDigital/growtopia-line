{{SNAPS_APART}}

Growtopia Snaps

A flat, semi-rigid single-dose pack. No cap, no tear strip: it opens along the line the precut system scores across it.

  1. Bend it into a V along the scored line, with the pack's face toward you.
  2. It snaps open at the score, at the point of the V.
  3. Pour with one hand, squeezing the two halves together.
